## Results narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016 – Pneumonia in Côte d'Ivoire

### Overall picture
No indicator on this results sheet has a calculable achievement. Most indicators have a target for July–September 2019 (or January–March 2020) but **no actual reported** for that period. Others have **no target**. The impact-level indicators have targets only for sub-groups and no overall figure. The figures below are the values recorded for July–September 2018. They are not measures of progress against the later targets.

### Health knowledge and promotion
The project aimed to reach 14,187 people with health promotion or behaviour change communication by the 2019 period, but no actual was reported for that period. Separate 2018 records show 9,031 and 13,798 people reached. The target for traditional healers reached was 28. Achievement for this indicator is not calculable because no actual was reported.

### Access to treatment and services
- **Volunteers trained:** target 127. A 2018 record shows 115.
- **Health staff trained:** target 6. 2018 records show 20.
- **Health centres supported:** target 4. A 2018 record shows 4.
- **Household visits:** the target was 2,539 households with children under 5 visited at least twice a month. 2018 records show 1,326 and 2,539.

All of these achievements are not calculable because no actual was reported for the target period.

### WASH
- **Water points:** target 2 for January–March 2020. A 2018 record shows 2.
- **Latrines:** baseline 519 in 2017 and target 1,000. 2018 records show 1,102.

Achievement for latrines is not calculable (no actual).

### Volunteers (HR)
- **Female volunteers:** target 55. A 2018 record shows 49.
- **Male volunteers:** target 72. 2018 records show 79.

### Indoor air quality
The baseline for households with an improved cooking stove was 47 in 2016, and the target was 1,500. A 2018 record shows 1,203. Achievement is not calculable (no actual).

### Knowledge and behaviour percentages
Several percentage indicators carried a target of 80 for 2019, with no actual reported. Examples are people seeking treatment in good time and people who know when to seek treatment. Correct handwashing had a target of 60 for 2020, also with no actual reported.

### Indicators without targets
Some indicators have no target, so achievement cannot be calculated. These include vaccination coverage for PCV and caretakers' knowledge of continued feeding.

### People reached (impact)
Achievement for people receiving direct resilience services is not calculable. The target is reported only by gender and age sub-groups, with no overall figure.
