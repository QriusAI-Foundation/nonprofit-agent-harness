# Results Narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016, Pneumonia in Cote d'Ivoire

This narrative summarises the results sheet for IATI activity NL-KVK-40409352-PRJ08-272-0005. For **every indicator, achievement against target is not calculable**. Most targets fall in the July to September 2019 or January to March 2020 periods, and no actual is reported for those periods. The figures below are reported actuals from the July to September 2018 period. They are shown next to targets for context only. They are not measures of achievement.

## Health promotion and knowledge

The project aimed to reach 14,187 people with health promotion or behaviour change communication. Reported 2018 entries for this indicator include 9,031 and 13,798. Achievement is not calculable because there is no actual. The project also targeted 28 traditional healers, and this achievement is likewise not calculable.

## Access to treatment and services

- **Volunteers:** the target was 127 trained and equipped volunteers. A 2018 actual of 115 is reported.
- **Health staff:** the target was 6 trained health staff. A 2018 actual of 20 is reported.
- **Health centres:** the target was 4 health centres supported. A 2018 actual of 4 is reported.
- **Household visits:** the target was 2,539 households with children under five visited at least twice per month. Reported 2018 entries include 1,326 and 2,539.
- **Transport and insurance:** the target was 5 communities with transport or insurance systems. Achievement is not calculable.

## WASH

Latrines started from a baseline of 519 in 2017 against a target of 1,000. A 2018 actual of 1,102 is reported. For water points, the target was 2, and a 2018 actual of 2 is reported. The handwashing target was 60%, and no actual is reported for that period.

## Indoor air quality

Households with an improved cooking stove started from a baseline of 47 in 2016 against a target of 1,500. A 2018 actual of 1,203 is reported.

## Volunteers by gender

The target was 55 female volunteers, with a 2018 actual of 49. The target was 72 male volunteers, with a 2018 actual of 79.

## Percentage outcomes

Targets of 80% were set for three outcomes:

- identifying danger signs of illness
- seeking treatment in time
- diversifying household diets

A target of 100% was set for exclusive breastfeeding. None of these has an actual for its target period, so achievement is not calculable.

Several indicators have no target at all, so their achievement is also not calculable. These include PCV vaccination, measles vaccination and child health cards.

## People reached

The resilience reach indicators cannot be totalled. Their targets are reported only by gender or by age and gender, with no overall figure.
