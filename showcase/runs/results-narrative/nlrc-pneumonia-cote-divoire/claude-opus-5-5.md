# Results narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016 – Pneumonia in Côte d'Ivoire

## Overview
Most targets for this project apply to the July–September 2019 period, but the sheet reports no actuals for that period. Achievement against target therefore cannot be calculated for any targeted indicator. The sheet gives the reason as no actual. Actuals reported for July–September 2018 appear below as reported. They have no stated targets and are not compared with the 2019 targets.

## Health promotion
The project set a target of 14187 people reached with health promotion and/or behaviour change communication, from a 2016 baseline of 0. Entries for July–September 2018 include actuals of 9031 and 13798.

## Access to treatment and services
- **Volunteers trained and equipped:** target 127. A 2018 entry records 115.
- **Health staff trained:** target 6. 2018 entries record 20.
- **Health centres supported:** target 4. A 2018 entry records 4.
- **Households with children under 5 visited at least twice per month:** target 2539. 2018 entries record 1326 and 2539.

## Water, sanitation and hygiene
- **Water points:** target 2 for January–March 2020. A 2018 entry records 2.
- **Latrines:** baseline 519 in 2017 and target 1000. 2018 entries record 1102.
- **Correct handwashing:** target 60 percent, with no actual reported.

## Indoor air quality
Households with and using an improved cooking stove started from a baseline of 47 in 2016 against a target of 1500. A 2018 entry records 1203.

## Volunteers
- **Female volunteers:** target 55. A 2018 entry records 49.
- **Male volunteers:** target 72. A 2018 entry records 79.

## Behaviour and knowledge outcomes
Several percentage indicators have 2019 targets but no reported actuals, so their achievement is not calculable:
- identifying danger signs of illness (80)
- diversification of household diet (80)
- timely treatment-seeking (80)
- exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months (100)

## Indicators without targets
Some indicators have no target, so achievement is not calculable. These include PCV and measles vaccination of children under 5. The impact indicators on people receiving direct services that contribute to resilience have no targets and no reported actuals.
