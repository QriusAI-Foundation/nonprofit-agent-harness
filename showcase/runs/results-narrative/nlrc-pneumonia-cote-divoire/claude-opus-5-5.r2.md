## Results narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016, Pneumonia in Cote d'Ivoire

### Overview
The results sheet sets targets for the 2019–2020 reporting periods, but **no actuals are reported for any target period**. As a result, the sheet marks achievement against target as **not calculable (no actual)** for every targeted indicator. This narrative therefore describes the targets alongside figures the sheet records for the July–September 2018 period. Those 2018 figures are not compared with targets, because the sheet itself makes no such comparison.

### Health knowledge and behaviour change
The target for people reached with health promotion and behaviour change communication was **14,187** for July–September 2019. No actual is reported for that period. The 2018 entries include **9,031** and **13,798**. The project also aimed to reach **28** traditional healers, with no actual reported. Several knowledge and behaviour indicators carry percentage targets with no reported actuals:

- identifying danger signs of illness: **80**
- timely treatment seeking: **80**
- knowing when to seek treatment: **80**
- household diet diversification: **80**
- exclusive breastfeeding: **100**
- correct handwashing: **60**, for January–March 2020

### Access to treatment and services
The volunteer target was **127** trained and equipped volunteers. A 2018 entry records **115**. Health staff training had a target of **6**, and 2018 entries record **20**. The target for health centres supported was **4**, and a 2018 entry also records **4**. For households with children under 5 visited at least twice a month, the target was **2,539**. The 2018 entries include **1,326** and **2,539**. Referral targets were also set but have no reported actuals:

- **140** referrals by trained volunteers
- **100** children with pneumonia symptoms taken to an appropriate provider
- **5** communities with transport or insurance systems

### Water, sanitation and indoor air
Two water points were targeted, and a 2018 entry records **2**. Latrines started from a baseline of **519**, against a target of **1,000**; 2018 entries record **1,102**. Households using improved cooking stoves started from a baseline of **47**, against a target of **1,500**; a 2018 entry records **1,203**.

### Volunteers
The target for female volunteers was **55**, and a 2018 entry records **49**. The target for male volunteers was **72**, and 2018 entries record **79**.

### Indicators without targets or totals
Several indicators have no target set, so their achievement is not calculable (no target). These include:

- child health cards
- PCV and measles vaccination coverage
- vaccination days
- community feedback systems

The impact indicators on people reached are also not calculable, for a different reason (no total). Their targets are reported only by gender or age group, with no overall figure.
