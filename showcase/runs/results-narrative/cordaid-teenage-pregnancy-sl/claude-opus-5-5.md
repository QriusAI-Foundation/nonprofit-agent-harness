# Results narrative: We Don cam Teenage Pregnancy Prevention in Sierra Leone

## Reach
The project reached **2084** direct beneficiaries in 2020 and **3400** in 2021. It reached **24130** indirect beneficiaries in 2020 and **23800** in 2021. No targets were stated, so achievement is not calculable for either indicator.

## Health services
In 2021, **5** health facilities were strengthened. Between July and September 2021, **1582** people had access to SRHR services.

**7** health centres had Youth Friendly Health Services in 2020, and **7** in 2021. **7** centres were assessed in 2020 and **7** in 2021. A target of 10 was set for March to July 2020, but no actual is reported for that period.

In 2021, **7** health facilities were supported or contracted, and **5** had an improved technical quality score. The percentage of facilities with an improved score in 2021 was **71.43**. The share of facilities with tracer medicines in stock was reported as **0** for 2021. A target of 50 was set for March 2020 to August 2021, with no actual reported.

## Governance and systems
The number of steering committees strengthened with action plans was **10** in April–June 2020 and **14** in July–September 2020. It was still **14** in July–September 2021.

Steering committees held **35** meetings in July–September 2021. A target of 48 was set for March 2020 to July 2021, with no actual reported.

**14** facilities had complete, age-specific registers in October–December 2020, and **14** in July–September 2021. A functional database was reported (**1**) in January–March 2021. **4** Family Support Units or police stations had complete SGBV case registers in April–June 2020, and **4** in July–September 2021.

**20** secondary schools had active peer and parent clubs in July–September 2020, and **20** in July–September 2021. There were **10** branded adolescent-friendly facilities in July–September 2020 and **2** in July–September 2021.

## Protection and justice
The number of young people and women receiving integrated SGBV services rose from **24** in January–March 2021 to **54** in July–September 2021.

SGBV cases investigated and referred within 7 days numbered **58** and then **87** over the same two quarters. Police patrols in grey areas numbered **79** and then **96**, and radio shows **2** and then **4**. Outreach sessions numbered **51** and then **97**.

Security institutions with RBF contracts went from **2** to **6**. **6** government institutions were involved in GBV prevention and response in July–September 2021.

## Reporting gaps
Several indicators have targets but no reported actuals. These results are unreported, not zero:

- Local staff trained (target 30)
- Religious leaders reached (target 80)
- Traditional leaders reached (target 150)
- People on short-term family planning methods (target 3000)
- Youth reached with targeted SRHR services (target 2800)
- Youth involved in SRHR activities (target 1200)
- People trained on gender norms (target 60)
- Family planning consultations (target 1600)
- Beneficiaries reached (HE) (target 4200)

Climate risk integration was reported as **0** for 2021. Achievement is recorded as not calculable (no target) for these indicators, including facilities supported.
