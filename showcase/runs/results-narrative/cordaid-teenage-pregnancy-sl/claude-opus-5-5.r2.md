## Results narrative: We Don cam Teenage Pregnancy Prevention in Sierra Leone

This narrative covers IATI activity NL-KVK-41160054-200782. The main finding is that the results sheet holds **no reported actual values**. Progress against targets therefore cannot be described. Missing actuals are not treated as zero; they are simply absent.

### Reach
At the Cordaid Overall level, direct beneficiaries have neither a target nor an actual for 2020. Achievement is recorded as not calculable (no target). Under Health Corporate Indicators, beneficiaries reached (HE) carried period targets of 100, 2,300 and 4,200, but no actuals were reported. Achievement is again not calculable (no target).

### Health services
The sheet sets these targets:

- 10 health centres with Youth Friendly Health Services.
- 3,000 people on modern short-term family planning methods, from a baseline of 0 in 2020.
- 300 youth making use of youth friendly health services.
- 1,200 youth involved in SRHR activities.
- 2,800 youth reached with targeted SRHR services.

Health facilities with an improved (technical) quality score had a baseline of 5 in 2021 and a target of 10. Health facilities supported or contracted had a baseline of 7 in 2021 and a target of 14. No actuals are reported for any of these, and the sheet records achievement as not calculable (no target). For family planning, it gives the same reason.

### Community engagement, advocacy and governance
Advocacy targets were 80 religious leaders and 150 community and traditional leaders. Community meetings and door-to-door work aimed to reach 450 people, and 30 local staff were to be trained in SRHR and meaningful youth participation. Governance targets included 16 strengthened steering committees, 48 quarterly steering committee meetings and 24 secondary schools with active peer and parent clubs. None has a reported actual.

### Security and justice
The targets were:

- 12 radio shows.
- 192 police foot and vehicular patrols in grey areas.
- 50 SGBV cases investigated and referred within 7 days.
- 2 security institutions with RBF contracts.
- 60 people trained on gender norms.

No actuals are reported.

### Women empowerment and climate resilience
The target for quarterly outreach sessions was 168, with no actual reported. For climate risk integration, neither a target nor an actual is given, and achievement is not calculable (no target).

### Data caveat
The sheet gives "no target" as the reason achievement is not calculable. It does so even for indicators that record a target over a multi-period window. A meaningful assessment of results will require actual values to be reported.
