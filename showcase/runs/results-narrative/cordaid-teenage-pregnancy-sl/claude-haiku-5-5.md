**We Don cam Teenage Pregnancy Prevention in Sierra Leone: results narrative** The results sheet records no calculable achievement for any indicator. For every line, no actual value has been reported, and a missing actual has not been treated as zero, so each achievement is marked not calculable. Under Cordaid Overall, the direct and indirect beneficiary indicators have no target stated for 2020 or 2021 and no actual reported. Both achievements are recorded as not calculable because no target is given. Under Quality Basic Services, the sheet lists access to SRHR services and strengthened health facilities for 2021. Neither has a target stated and no actual is reported, so neither achievement can be assessed. Health Thematic Indicators holds the largest set of planned targets. Youth making use of youth friendly health services has a target of 300 for March 2020 to July 2020. The family planning indicator for modern short-term methods has a target of 3000 for March 2020 to July 2021, and its baseline is recorded as 0 in 2020. Targets are also set for 30 local staff trained, 80 religious leaders reached and 1200 youth involved in SRHR activities, but none of these has a reported actual. Health Project Specific Indicators sets a target of 24 secondary schools with active peer and parent clubs on TPP and a target of 12 health facilities with complete registers. No actuals are reported for either. Under Security & Justice Project Specific Indicators, the target for radio shows broadcast is 12 and the target for patrols in grey areas is 192, both for March 2020 to August 2021. Neither has a reported actual. Health Corporate Indicators sets a target of 4200 beneficiaries reached for July 2020 to July 2021 and 2800 youth reached with targeted SRHR services. Health facilities supported or contracted have a baseline of 7 in 2021 and a target of 14. No actuals are reported. Under Climate Resilience, the project's integration of climate risks has no target stated and no actual reported for 2021, so its achievement is not calculable. In short, the sheet cannot show progress against any target. Until actual values are reported for each period, no result in this report can be judged as achieved or missed.
