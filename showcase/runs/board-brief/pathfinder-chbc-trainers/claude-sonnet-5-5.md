# Board Brief: CHBC Trainer's Guide (Pathfinder International, 2006)

**What it is.** This is a training guide for trainers. It teaches community health workers (CHWs) to give home-based care to people living with HIV/AIDS. Pathfinder International published it in October 2006. It is meant to be adapted to local needs.

**What it asks of an organisation.** It sets out how to run the training and how to select and support the CHWs. The key points are below.

## Six key points

1. **Home care has a limited role.** The guide says home-based care is not a replacement for hospital care. It is one part of a wider set of services.
2. **Keep classes small.** The guide limits each training to 15-20 participants. This leaves time to practise skills.
3. **Let the community choose CHWs.** The guide says CHWs should be selected by the community, with help from local community and faith-based groups.
4. **Plan for ongoing supervision.** The guide says supervision and support are needed after training. This helps CHWs with challenges.
5. **The course can be adapted.** Units can be combined into a shorter course. This depends on time, funding and local needs.
6. **Protect clients during practice.** Real clients take part in the practicum. They must be told they can refuse. Refusing will not affect the care they already receive.

## What the board should watch

- Whether we have the trainers, supervisors and time to meet these standards.
- Whether CHWs are chosen with community input.
- Whether client consent is handled properly if we run practice visits.
- Whether a shorter course would still cover what our setting needs.

The board should ask staff to say which of these points we can already meet, and which need a decision from the board.

## Evidence cited

   > "CHBC is not a replacement for hospital care." (Notes to the Trainer)
2. Trainings should be limited to 15-20 participants to allow good skills practice.
   > "should be limited to 15-20 participants to ensure a good trainer-participant ratio" (Notes to the Trainer: Training and Supervision of Community Health Workers)
3. CHWs should be selected by the community, with help from community-based and faith-based organisations.
   > "should be selected by the community, with the help of CBOs and/or FBOs." (Notes to the Trainer: CHW Selection Process)
4. Ongoing supervision and support are needed to help CHWs overcome challenges.
   > "Ongoing supervision and support is needed to help the CHW overcome challenges," (Notes to the Trainer: Training and Supervision of Community Health Workers)
