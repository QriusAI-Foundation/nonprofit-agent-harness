# Board Brief: Pathfinder CHBC Trainer's Guide (2006)

**What it is.** This is a training course from Pathfinder International, dated October 2006. Trainers use it to prepare community health workers (CHWs) to give home-based care to people living with HIV/AIDS. It covers HIV basics, nursing care, nutrition, communication, record keeping and community mobilisation. There is a matching Handbook for participants.

## Key points

1. **Purpose.** The course gives CHWs the knowledge and skills to care for people with HIV/AIDS in their own communities.
2. **Size and shape.** There are 13 core units, a practicum, and 5 advanced units. The advanced units cover topics such as family planning and antiretroviral therapy (ART). A full course is a big commitment, but the guide says units can be combined into shorter courses.
3. **Free to adapt.** We may copy or adapt any part without asking first. In return, we must credit Pathfinder and must not sell it for profit.
4. **Fair selection of CHWs.** The guide warns against choosing CHWs because they are friends or relatives of the project manager. The board should make sure our selection is open and fair.
5. **Client consent in the practicum.** Trainees practise on real clients at home. Clients must know they can say no, and that saying no will not affect their care. The board should check that consent is obtained properly.
6. **Ongoing supervision.** Training alone is not enough. CHWs need continued supervision and support to keep service quality high.

## What the board should decide or watch

- **Adopt and adapt?** Decide whether to use this guide and which units fit our needs.
- **Resources.** Budget for trainers, materials and supervision after training ends.
- **Safeguards.** Watch that CHW selection is fair and that client consent is respected.
- **Credit and pricing.** If we adapt the guide, credit Pathfinder and share it free or at cost.

## Evidence cited

1. The curriculum aims to give CHWs the knowledge and skills to provide CHBC to PLWHA in their communities.
   > "This curriculum aims to provide CHWs with the knowledge and skills necessary to provide CHBC to PLWHA in their communities." (Introduction to the Curriculum)
2. The guide has 13 core units, a practicum, and 5 advanced units.
   > "The Trainer’s Guide is divided into 13 core units, a practicum, and 5 advanced units that contain additional information that CHWs might find useful after they have covered the core areas." (Introduction to the Curriculum)
3. The material may be reproduced or adapted without permission if Pathfinder is credited and it is free or at cost.
   > "Any part of this document may be reproduced or adapted to meet local needs without prior permission from Pathfinder International provided that Pathfinder International is acknowledged, and the material is made available free of charge or at cost." (Copyright notice)
4. CHW selection should avoid favoritism or patronage.
   > "Care should be taken to avoid favoritism or patronage (e.g., getting the job because you are related to or friends with the project manager)." (Pathfinder’s CHBC Model – CHW Selection Process)
5. Clients can refuse to take part in the practicum without it affecting their care.
   > "Clients should understand that they can refuse to participate in the practicum and that it will not affect the care that they are already receiving." (More About the CHBC Practicum, Evaluation, and Certification)
6. CHWs need ongoing supervision and support to ensure good quality services.
   > "Ongoing supervision and support is needed to help the CHW overcome challenges, to ensure that PLWHA are getting good quality services, and to facilitate learning between trainings." (Training and Supervision of Community Health Workers)
