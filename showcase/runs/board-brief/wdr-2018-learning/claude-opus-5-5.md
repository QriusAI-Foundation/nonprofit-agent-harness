# Board Brief: World Development Report 2018 – Learning to Realize Education's Promise

## What this document is
This is the World Bank's 2018 World Development Report. It is the first in the series to focus fully on education. It is a global report. It does not place direct duties on our organisation. It does give a clear view of what works in education, and we can use that view to check our own programmes.

## Six key points

1. **A first for education.** The report is the first in the series devoted entirely to education. That gives its findings weight with funders and partners.

2. **School is not the same as learning.** Many children attend school but learn little. The report's core message is that time in class does not guarantee skills.

3. **The poorest children are hit hardest.** Poor children learn the least, and this hurts them the most. Programmes that reach disadvantaged children matter most.

4. **Measure learning.** The report's first strategy is to assess learning and make it a serious goal. Without good measures, the problem stays hidden.

5. **Teachers are central.** Both teacher skills and teacher motivation affect learning.

6. **How money is used matters.** The report stresses that extra resources must be used in ways that improve learning.

## What the board should decide or watch
- **Decide** whether our programmes track what children actually learn, not just how many attend.
- **Decide** whether we put enough focus on the poorest and most excluded children.
- **Watch** whether we support teachers' skills and motivation.
- **Watch** that new spending is tied to learning results, not just to more of the same.

## Bottom line
The report asks everyone in education to put learning at the centre. Our board can use it as a checklist for our own work.
