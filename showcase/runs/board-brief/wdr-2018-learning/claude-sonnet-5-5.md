# Board brief: World Development Report 2018, *Learning to Realize Education's Promise*

## What this is
This is a long report from the World Bank. It is about education systems in countries. It is not written for small nonprofits. It makes no request of our organisation. It speaks to countries and their education systems.

## Six key points

1. **Who made it.** The report is a product of World Bank staff with outside contributions. Its views may not match those of the Bank itself.

2. **The problem.** Many children are not learning, and many are not in school at all. The report cites about 260 million children and youth not enrolled.

3. **First step: assess learning.** Countries should measure and track learning better. They should then use the results to guide action.

4. **Second step: act on evidence.** Countries should use evidence to guide innovation and practice in schools.

5. **Third step: align the system.** Countries must tackle the technical and political barriers to learning at scale.

6. **A warning.** The report says taking learning seriously will not be easy.

## What the board might do
Nothing in the report requires a board decision. If our work touches education, the board could ask staff three questions:
- Do we measure whether people we serve are actually learning?
- Do we use evidence when we pick our methods?
- Do our partners and funders pull in the same direction?

Watch for any stronger claims being made on the report's behalf. It sets out ideas for countries. It is not a rulebook for small groups.
