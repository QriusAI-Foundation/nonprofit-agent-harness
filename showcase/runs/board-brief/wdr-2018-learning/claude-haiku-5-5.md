# Board Brief: World Development Report 2018, Learning to Realize Education's Promise

## What this document is

This is the 2018 World Development Report from the World Bank. It is the first in this series devoted entirely to education. It argues that schooling is not the same as learning, and that many children finish school without basic skills.

## What it asks of organisations

The report asks governments and partners to put learning first. It gives three strategies. Measure learning well. Use evidence to improve teaching in classrooms. Align the whole system around learning.

## Key points

1. **Schooling is not learning.** Many children attend school for years but still cannot read a simple sentence or do basic arithmetic. The report calls this a learning crisis.

2. **Poor and marginalized children learn least.** Gaps by income, gender, disability and location are large. The report says the gaps often grow as children move up through school.

3. **Measure learning to make it visible.** Many countries lack reliable data on what children learn. Without it, problems stay hidden and reform is hard to start.

4. **Early years matter.** Poor nutrition, stress and weak early stimulation hurt brain development. Children who start behind often stay behind.

5. **Teachers and classroom practice drive results.** Teacher skill, motivation and teaching at the right level for each child make a clear difference. Inputs such as technology or books help only when they support teaching and learning.

6. **Reform must work across the whole system.** Technical barriers and competing interests can pull education systems away from learning. Reforms need coalitions, feedback, and room to adapt.

## What the board should decide or watch

- Ask whether our work has any measure of learning, not only attendance or enrolment.
- Ask which children we serve who are most at risk of falling behind.
- Watch for quick fixes that add inputs without changing classroom teaching.
- Consider whether our partners can show learning results, not only activity.
- Keep the report on the agenda when reviewing education programmes.

## Evidence cited

1. Schooling is not the same as learning; many children in school still cannot read a simple sentence.
   > "Schooling is not the same as learning." (OVERVIEW: Learning to realize education's promise)
2. The report calls the gap between schooling and learning a learning crisis.
   > "This learning crisis is a moral crisis." (OVERVIEW: Learning to realize education's promise)
3. Learning outcomes are almost always much worse for disadvantaged children.
   > "Within countries, learning outcomes are almost always much worse for the disadvantaged." (OVERVIEW: Learning to realize education's promise)
4. Many countries lack the information needed to measure learning outcomes.
   > "Many countries lack information on even basic reading and math competencies." (Assess learning—to make it a serious goal)
5. Children from disadvantaged backgrounds show learning deficits before they start school.
   > "Children from disadvantaged backgrounds tend to exhibit learning deficits years before they start school" (Chapter 3: Children do not arrive ready to learn)
6. Teachers are the most important factor affecting learning in schools.
   > "Teachers are the most important factor affecting learning in schools." (OVERVIEW: The three dimensions of the learning crisis)
7. Systems must align actors and overcome technical and political barriers to make learning work at scale.
   > "Countries must recognize that all the classroom innovation in the world is unlikely to have much impact if, because of technical and political barriers, the system as a whole does not support learning." (OVERVIEW: Learning to realize education's promise)
