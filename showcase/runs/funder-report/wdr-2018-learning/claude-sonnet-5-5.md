## Programme approach and training content

The programme's training material is the World Bank's *World Development Report 2018: Learning to Realize Education's Promise*. This section describes what that material covers. It reports no outcomes for participants, because the source document gives none.

### Organising framework

The material is organised around four main themes. These are education's promise, the need to shine a light on learning, how to make schools work for all learners, and how to make systems work for learning.

The Overview condenses the content into three complementary strategies:

- **Assess learning.** The material calls for measuring and tracking learning better and using the results to guide action.
- **Act on evidence.** It advises using evidence to guide innovation and practice.
- **Align actors.** It focuses on tackling the technical and political barriers to learning at scale.

### Content at school and classroom level

Several chapters turn the second strategy into specific guidance:

- **Learner preparation.** The material recommends fostering cognitive and socioemotional development through early childhood interventions. It also says remedial education can work if it reaches the right people using the right approach.
- **Teacher training.** The material states that training needs to be individually targeted and repeated, with follow-up coaching.
- **School inputs.** The material advises that other inputs, including new technology, should complement teachers.

### Content at system level

For the third strategy, the material names three sets of tools that reformers can use to shift a system toward learning.

### Adaptation to context

The material stresses that guidance has to be adapted to local conditions. In discussing borrowed reforms, it says home-grown, context-specific solutions are important.
