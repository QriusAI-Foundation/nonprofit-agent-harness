## Programme approach and training content

The programme's volunteer training draws on the *Volunteer Manual for Community-based health and first aid in action (CBHFA)*, published by the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies and dated March 2009. This section describes the approach and content set out in that manual. It does not report programme outcomes.

### Approach

The manual describes an integrated approach that trains and mobilizes volunteers from the community to carry out activities in the community. Its method is summarized as "learn in the classroom: do in the community." The manual identifies community participation as the core of CBHFA. It states that communities should be involved at every stage of the work. The programme is described as flexible.

### Structure of the curriculum

The first three modules are obligatory. Through a community assessment, the community decides on volunteers' priority learning needs. In the remaining modules, some topics are obligatory and many are optional.

- **Module 3 (assessment-based action)** includes the identification and prioritization of health, first aid and safety issues. Three to five priorities are selected for CBHFA activities, and an action plan is developed with the community health committee and local branch staff. The manual also states that volunteers complete a report form after each household visit or whenever CBHFA activities are performed.
- **Module 4 (basic first aid and injury prevention)** contains 20 topics, five of them optional. It teaches four steps for first aid action: assess, plan, implement and evaluate. The manual specifies that basic life support skills must be trained by qualified first aid trainers recognized by the National Society.
- **Module 5** covers community mobilization in major emergencies and public health in emergencies, including preventing and responding to epidemics.
- **Module 6 (disease prevention and health promotion)** has 16 topics, of which volunteers choose at least five according to the priorities set in the community assessment. The manual states that behaviour change communication is most effective when it uses simple messages.
- **Module 7** addresses supplementary topics for community education and assistance.

### Materials

Volunteers receive the Volunteer Manual to take home. They also use the Community Tools, a set of dialogue cue cards for household and community members. These cards provide a visual aid for communicating with community members. Before the assessment, a sensitization meeting introduces CBHFA to the community.
