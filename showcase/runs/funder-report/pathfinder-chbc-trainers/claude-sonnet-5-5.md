## Programme approach and training content

This section describes the training material used by the programme, Pathfinder International's *Community Home-Based Care for People and Communities Affected by HIV/AIDS: A Comprehensive Training Course for Community Health Workers* (Trainer's Guide, 2006). It describes the design of the curriculum only. The guide does not report results.

### Purpose and definition

The guide defines community home-based care (CHBC) as care and support of people living with HIV/AIDS (PLWHA) in their own homes and communities. It states that the curriculum aims to give community health workers (CHWs) the knowledge and skills to provide CHBC. It says the CHW's main responsibility should be to transfer nursing and support skills to caregivers.

### Structure

The Trainer's Guide has 13 core units, a practicum and 5 advanced units. The core training covers skills in basic nursing, among other areas. Each unit has learning objectives, activities and trainer's tools.

### Training approach

The guide describes the programme as participatory and based on adult learning principles. It says content should be practical, with a focus on learning by doing. It recommends limiting each training to 15-20 participants so there is enough time for skills practice. It tells trainers to adapt activities to participants' literacy levels, for example by using pictures or speaking in place of reading and writing.

### Practicum, evaluation and consent

The guide includes three practicums covering client care and community mobilization. Before the home-based practicum, it says informed consent must be obtained from PLWHA who are willing to be cared for by participants. Pre- and post-tests are given to all participants.

### Development

According to the guide, the curriculum was pre-tested in Tanzania and Mozambique and underwent internal and external peer review.
