## Programme approach and training content

The Vietnam Escuela Nueva (VNEN) programme is a whole-school reform of teaching and learning that the source document describes as integrating several practices: participative and collaborative learning, self-paced learning guides, student government, formative assessment combined with summative assessment, application or real-life oriented learning with community integration, and teacher professional networks. The source states that the combination of these elements is intended to spur a transformative learning experience.

The programme draws on the Escuela Nueva model that began in Colombia in the 1970s. The source notes that Escuela Nueva used self-paced learning guides instead of textbooks, and that the programme was adapted for Vietnam with learning guides developed by Vietnamese textbook experts. A pilot began in 2010 across six provinces.

The learning guide is central to classroom practice. According to the source, learning guides replace textbooks under the VNEN model and combine a lessons section with a workbook of exercises and sequenced instructions. Teachers are trained to adjust the learning guide's activities. The qualitative study reports that teachers rarely change guide content but often adjust the types or sequence of activities.

Training was delivered in a specific format. According to the source, all primary school teachers in Vietnam receive two weeks of summer training, which constituted the initial VNEN training for most teachers. The source states that the training event consisted of practical implementation of VNEN classrooms rather than lectures, and that teachers were involved at all levels of training, including as trainers. Teachers received training materials at least two weeks before the training event.

Sustained professional development was also part of the design. The source describes biweekly cluster meetings for teachers. It reports that VNEN teachers attended on average three to four cluster meetings per year, against an expected figure of closer to 15 or 16, and that the biweekly cluster training was not fully implemented in some schools.

The source reports that control group teachers also attended VNEN training, with the share answering yes rising from 37% in 2013 to 71% in 2015, and that the training was not mandatory for non-VNEN schools.

The source reports variation in implementation across schools. It notes that the classroom artifacts and parental engagement elements were used less consistently than other elements, and that implementation intensity varied across schools and teachers.

## Evidence cited

1. The VNEN programme integrates several practices, including participative and collaborative learning, self-paced learning guides, and student government.
   > "The VNEN incorporates and integrates several innovative and globally recognized practices including: (a) participative and collaborative learning; (b) self-paced learning guides; (c) student government;" (Executive Summary)
2. The source states that the combination of VNEN elements is intended to spur a transformative and powerful learning experience.
   > "The combination of these elements is intended to spur a transformative and powerful learning experience." (Executive Summary)
3. Escuela Nueva began in Colombia in the 1970s and used self-paced learning guides instead of textbooks.
   > "Escuela Nueva began to be implemented in Colombia in the 1970s as a program that transformed rural education in that South American country." (1.1.1 Origins in Colombia)
4. Escuela Nueva included self-paced learning guides instead of textbooks.
   > "These included self-paced learning guides instead of textbooks and a flexible school calendar adapted to imperatives of rural life" (1.1.1 Origins in Colombia)
5. Learning guides for Vietnamese children were developed in Vietnam by Vietnamese textbook experts.
   > "On a practical plane, learning guides for Vietnamese children needed to be developed for Vietnam by Vietnamese textbook experts." (Executive Summary)
6. A pilot of Escuela Nueva in Vietnam began implementation in 2010.
   > "to design a pilot of Escuela Nueva in Vietnam that began implementation in 2010." (1.1.2 Adaptation in Vietnam)
7. Learning guides replace textbooks under the VNEN model.
   > "Learning Guides (LGs) replace textbooks under the VNEN model." (4.1.3 Learning Guides)
