# Results narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016, Pneumonia in Cote d'Ivoire

Activity NL-KVK-40409352-PRJ08-272-0005

## Overall picture

The results sheet does not allow an achievement figure to be calculated for any indicator. Most indicators have a target for a later period but no reported actual. The rest have no target. Missing actuals are not treated as zero. Where the sheet records actuals, they appear in 2018 periods with no target stated, so they cannot be set against the later targets.

## Health: reach and access

For people reached with health promotion and behaviour change communication, the baseline is 0 in 2016. The target for July to September 2019 is 14187, and no actual is reported for that period. Separate 2018 entries record actuals of 9031 and 13798, with no target stated. The achievement is not calculable because there is no actual.

For volunteers trained and equipped to perform community health services, the 2019 target is 127 and no actual is reported. A 2018 entry records an actual of 115. For health staff trained, the 2019 target is 6 with no actual reported, and 2018 entries record an actual of 20. For households with children under 5 visited at least twice per month by volunteers, the 2019 target is 2539 with no actual reported, while a 2018 entry records an actual of 2539. All of these achievements are not calculable because there is no actual.

For health centers supported, the 2019 target is 4 and no actual is reported, though a 2018 entry records an actual of 4.

The knowledge indicators follow the same pattern. People identifying danger signs of illness has a 2019 target of 80 and no actual reported. Several indicators have a baseline but no target at all, such as caretakers knowing that continued feeding and drinking of an ill child is crucial.

## Water, sanitation and cooking

For latrines constructed or rehabilitated, the baseline is 519 in 2017 and the 2019 target is 1000, with no actual reported for that period. A 2018 entry records an actual of 1102. For water points constructed or rehabilitated, the 2020 target is 2 and no actual is reported, while a 2018 entry records an actual of 2. For correct handwashing, the target is 60 and no actual is reported.

For households that have and use an improved cooking stove, the baseline is 47 in 2016 and the 2019 target is 1500, with no actual reported. A 2018 entry records an actual of 1203.

## Volunteers and resilience

A 2018 entry records 49 female volunteers, against a 2019 target of 55 with no actual reported. A 2018 entry records 79 male volunteers, against a 2019 target of 72 with no actual reported.

The resilience indicators for people reached have a baseline of 0 in 2017. They state no targets and report no actuals, so their achievement is not calculable.
