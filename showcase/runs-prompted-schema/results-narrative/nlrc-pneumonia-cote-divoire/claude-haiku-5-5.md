## Results narrative: 3FM Serious Request 2016, Pneumonia in Cote d'Ivoire

This narrative covers the results recorded for IATI activity NL-KVK-40409352-PRJ08-272-0005. Achievement cannot be calculated for the indicators below, and the sheet gives a reason for each. Where the sheet records no actual for a target period, this narrative reports that no actual is reported and does not treat the gap as zero.

### Health knowledge and promotion

For people reached with health promotion and/or behaviour change communication, the 2019 target is 14187 and no actual is reported. Entries from the 2018 period show reported actuals of 9031 and 13798, but no target is stated for those entries, so the achievement is not calculable.

For traditional healers reached through the project, the 2019 target is 28 with no actual reported. The achievement is not calculable because no actual is available.

For caretakers' knowledge of continued feeding and drinking, the sheet gives no target, so the achievement is not calculable.

### Health seeking and referrals

Referrals of ill community members by RCRC trained volunteers have a 2019 target of 140, with no actual reported. For children under 5 with pneumonia symptoms taken to an appropriate health care provider, the 2019 target is 100 and no actual is reported.

### Access to treatment and services

Volunteers trained and equipped to perform community health services show a 2018 actual of 115, but no target is stated for that entry. Health staff trained have a 2019 target of 6 with no actual reported, while 2018 entries report 20. Health centers supported have a 2019 target of 4 with no actual reported, and a 2018 entry reports 4.

For households with children under 5 visited at least twice per month, the 2019 target is 2539 with no actual reported. The 2018 entries report 1326 and 2539, with no target stated.

### WASH

For water points constructed or rehabilitated, the 2020 target is 2 with no actual reported. For latrines constructed or rehabilitated, the sheet gives a 2017 baseline of 519 and a 2019 target of 1000, with no actual reported; a 2018 entry reports 1102. For people practicing correct handwashing, the 2020 target is 60 with no actual reported.

### Human resources

For female volunteers working in the project, the 2019 target is 55 and a 2018 entry reports 49. For male volunteers, the 2019 target is 72 with no actual reported.

### Other outcomes

For households that have and use an improved cooking stove, the 2016 baseline is 47 and the 2019 target is 1500. A 2018 entry reports 1203. For mothers practising exclusive breastfeeding for the first six months, the 2019 target is 100 with no actual reported.

### Indicators without a target

Several indicators have no target, including child health cards, PCV and measles vaccination, vaccination days, and project feedback systems, so their achievement is not calculable. Indicators on people receiving resilience services record no target and none reported.
