# We Don cam Teenage Pregnancy Prevention in Sierra Leone: Results Narrative

## Reach
Under Cordaid Overall, the activity reported 2084 direct beneficiaries in 2020 and 3400 in 2021. Indirect beneficiaries were 24130 in 2020 and 23800 in 2021. No targets are stated for these indicators, so the sheet records achievement as not calculable (no target).

## Health services
In July to September 2021, 1582 people had access to SRHR services. Five health facilities were strengthened in 2021. Seven health centres with Youth Friendly Health Services were reported in 2021. The sheet lists a target of 10 for an earlier period but no actual was reported against it. The share of facilities with tracer medicines and commodities in stock was reported as 0 for 2021. The share of health facilities with an improved technical quality score in 2021 was 71.43 percent.

## Steering committees, schools and SGBV services
The number of multi-stakeholder steering committees strengthened with action plans was 10 in April to June 2020 and 14 in July to September 2021. In July to September 2021, 35 quarterly steering committee meetings were conducted. Twenty secondary schools had active peer and parent clubs on teenage pregnancy prevention in the same quarter. A total of 54 young people and women of reproductive age received an integrated package of SGBV services in July to September 2021. Branded adolescent friendly facilities were reported at 10 in July to September 2020 and 2 in July to September 2021.

## Security and justice
In July to September 2021, four radio shows were broadcast and police carried out 96 patrols in grey areas. In the same quarter, 87 SGBV cases were reported as investigated and referred within 7 days. Six security institutions held RBF contracts.

## Women empowerment
The activity held 97 quarterly outreach sessions in July to September 2021.

## Gaps and limits
Several indicators have targets but no reported actuals. The sheet lists a target of 30 local staff trained in SRHR and youth participation, with no actual reported. It lists a target of 2800 youth reached with targeted SRHR services, also with no actual reported. These missing actuals are not treated as zero. The climate resilience indicator, on whether the project integrates climate risks in programming, was reported as 0 percent for 2021.

Every achievement on the sheet is marked not calculable because no target is stated. This narrative therefore reports actual figures only and does not say whether any target was met.
