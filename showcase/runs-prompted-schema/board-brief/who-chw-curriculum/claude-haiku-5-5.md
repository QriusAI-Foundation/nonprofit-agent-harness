# Board brief: Global curriculum guide for community health workers (WHO, 2025)

**What it is.** A World Health Organization guide for designing and running training for community health workers (CHWs). It is a foundation to adapt. It is not a ready-made course.

**Key points**

1. **Purpose.** It helps plan CHW curricula. The aim is to strengthen essential services at community level as part of primary health care.

2. **Who it is mainly for.** CHWs who are formally part of the health system. These CHWs have a structured training path, a contract and proper pay. Volunteers are not the main focus.

3. **Main readers.** CHW educators and people who write CHW curricula. Managers, supervisors and funders are secondary readers.

4. **Structure.** Six competency domains, 12 universal modules and four role-specific modules. The role-specific modules hold 28 topics.

5. **Adapt, do not copy.** Tailor the content to local health systems, needs and CHW roles. Use only the role-specific topics that fit your setting.

6. **Review.** Update the curriculum regularly. The guide suggests at least every 5 to 10 years.

**What the board might decide or watch**

- Do our CHW roles fit the guide's scope? Volunteers may not fit the target group.
- Who would own the adaptation work? The guide says it should involve CHWs, employers and government.
- Do we have a plan and budget for regular curriculum review?
- Does the guide suggest the right competencies for our staff? Check before relying on it.

The guide itself warns that no single CHW can do every task in its role-specific modules. Choose carefully.

## Evidence cited

1. The guide was written to help shape CHW curricula.
   > "This guide was developed to assist in the conceptualization, design and implementation of CHW curricula" (Executive summary)
2. The guide is designed mainly for formally integrated CHWs with a structured training path and a contract.
   > "This curriculum guide is designed mainly for CHWs, who are formally integrated into the health system after a structured education pathway" (Executive summary)
3. The main readers are CHW educators and people developing CHW curricula.
   > "The main target audience for this document comprises CHW educators as well those developing CHW curricula." (Executive summary)
4. The guide has six competency domains, 12 universal modules and four role-specific modules.
   > "The content is organized into six competency domains, 12 universal modules (11 practice activities plus one module of basic science knowledge), and four role-specific modules, comprising 28 role-specific topics organized under the four modules." (3.1 Overview of the CHW curriculum guide)
5. The guide should be adapted to different countries, health systems and contexts.
   > "This CHW curriculum guide should be adapted to the needs of different countries, health systems, programmes and contexts." (Executive summary)
6. The curriculum should be updated regularly, for example at least every 5 to 10 years.
   > "deliver, evaluate and update the new curriculum as regularly as needed (indicated by programme evaluation) or periodically, for example at least every 5–10 years." (3.3 How to use the curriculum guide)
