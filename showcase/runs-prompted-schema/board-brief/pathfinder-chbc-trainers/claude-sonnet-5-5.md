# Board Brief: CHBC Trainer's Guide (Pathfinder International, October 2006)

## What this is
This is a training guide for trainers. It teaches community health workers (CHWs) to give home-based care to people living with HIV/AIDS. Pathfinder International published it in October 2006.

## What it asks of us
If we use this guide, we take on the way of working it sets out. That covers how we pick CHWs, how we run training, and how we treat clients.

## Six key points
1. **Purpose.** The guide aims to give CHWs the knowledge and skills to provide home-based care in their own communities.
2. **Layout.** It has core units, a practicum, and advanced units. The advanced units are for CHWs who have finished the core.
3. **Role.** Home care does not replace hospital care.
4. **Class size.** Training groups should stay small, so everyone gets time to practise.
5. **Choosing CHWs.** Selection must be fair and free of favouritism.
6. **Client consent.** Clients can refuse to take part in the practicum. Their care must not suffer if they refuse.

## What the board should decide or watch
- **Decide:** whether we can limit each training to 15-20 people.
- **Watch:** that CHW selection stays fair and is not based on personal ties.
- **Watch:** that every client gives informed consent before any practicum visit.
- **Ask staff:** how we will deliver the core units, the practicum, and any advanced units we choose.

## Evidence cited

1. The guide aims to give CHWs the knowledge and skills to provide home-based care to people living with HIV/AIDS in their communities.
   > "This curriculum aims to provide CHWs with the knowledge and skills necessary to provide CHBC to PLWHA in their communities." (Notes to the Trainer: Introduction to the Curriculum)
2. The guide has core units, a practicum, and advanced units meant for use after the core areas are covered.
   > "The Trainer’s Guide is divided into 13 core units, a practicum, and 5 advanced units that contain additional information that CHWs might find useful after they have covered the core areas." (Notes to the Trainer: Introduction to the Curriculum)
3. Home-based care does not replace hospital care.
   > "CHBC is not a replacement for hospital care." (Notes to the Trainer)
4. Training groups should be limited to 15-20 participants so there is a good trainer ratio and time for skills practice.
   > "CHBC trainings should be limited to 15-20 participants to ensure a good trainer-participant ratio and adequate time for skills practice." (Notes to the Trainer: Training and Supervision of Community Health Workers)
5. CHW selection must avoid favouritism and patronage.
   > "Care should be taken to avoid favoritism or patronage (e.g., getting the job because you are related to or friends with the project manager)." (Notes to the Trainer: CHW Selection Process)
6. Clients can refuse to take part in the practicum, and refusing must not affect the care they already receive.
   > "Clients should understand that they can refuse to participate in the practicum and that it will not affect the care that they are already receiving." (Notes to the Trainer: More About the CHBC Practicum, Evaluation, and Certification)
