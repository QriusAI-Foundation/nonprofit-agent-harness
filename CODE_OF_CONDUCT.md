# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
and publishing others' private information.

This project serves organisations working with vulnerable people. That context applies
to how we talk about the people our software touches too: no one who appears in a
dataset, a case note, or an example is a prop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **sanmaya@qriusai.org**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block accounts.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), v2.1.
